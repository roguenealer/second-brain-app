Second Brain

A private, personal knowledge tool.

Second Brain runs on its owner's own computer. It keeps the owner's notes about people, projects and plans in order, writes a short daily briefing, and does this with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic).

It is used only by its owner. It isn't offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What it does with Google

  • Reads the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, to keep the owner's notes current.
  • Sends an email only when the owner has approved that specific email.
  • Keeps backups of the owner's notes in the owner's own Google Drive.
